# Algorithms

    A set of instructions to perform a task or to solve a given problem.

## Example

    A recipe book is a collection of recipes in which each recipe provide an step by step instruction to prepare food.

        Let's say you want to prepare a tea. 

    So, the steps would be -

        1. Boil water
        2. Put tea in tea pot
        3. Add hot water.
        4. Put hot tea into tea cups
        5. Do you need sugar?
            a. If yes, put it into tea cups
            b. If no, do nothing
        6. Stir, drink and enjoy !!!
